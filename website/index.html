Rule 01

Selection is scale + inversion, never a border

The biggest tell. A focused tile gets no outline — it grows ~4–8%, gains a soft shadow, and flips to the opposite luminance: the focused row turns solid white with dark text. Outline-only focus reads as "generic desktop app".

Rule 02

One saturated hue owns the screen

Each context commits to one brand colour — vibrant accent, dashboard blue, software pages near-black. Depth comes from tinting that same hue (white at 8–15% alpha), never a second unrelated grey. Two mismatched greys is the classic mistake.

Rule 03

Nested, decreasing corner radii

Outer container ~20–24px, cards ~14–16px, thumbnails ~8–12px, chips fully round. Radius always decreases as you nest inward. Nothing is a sharp rectangle except artwork.

Rule 04

A persistent button legend, bottom-right

Every screen ends in a floating pill: circular glyphs — Ⓐ Ⓑ Ⓧ Ⓨ — each followed by a verb ("Confirm", "Back"). Always docked, it replaces scrollbars, tooltips and menu bars as the primary affordance.

Rule 05

Two icon vocabularies, strictly separated

System icons are monochrome, uniform-stroke line art, optically equal in size, inheriting the foreground colour. Content — box art, avatars — is the only full-colour element. Coloured system icons destroy the look.

Rule 06

Circular icon buttons

Perfect circles, neutral tinted fill, centred glyph about half the circle's diameter. The active one inverts: white fill, coloured glyph.

Click to invert and test
Rule 07

Quiet status cluster, top corner

Time, Wi-Fi, battery sit top-right — small, low-contrast, never bold. Battery is a drawn pill with a terminal nub. Status never competes with content.

75%
Adjust to witness low charge red transition below 15%
Rule 08

Rounded humanist sans, big weight jumps

Titles are heavy (600–700) and clearly larger; body is regular and dimmed to 60–70% opacity. Hierarchy comes from weight and size, not colour or rules.

Heavy Title (700) 28px
Medium Subhead (600) 16px
Regular Body (400) 14px dimmed to 65% opacity. Notice how no secondary colors or underlines are needed for hierarchy.
Rule 09

Content peeks and breathes

Carousels cut adjacent items off at the edges so the shelf reads as continuous. Padding is generous — about one icon-width around every group.

Rule 10

Flat fills; motion supplies the life

No gradients or bevels. Richness lives in movement: eased scale on focus, springy press, inertial scrolling. 130–260ms, ease-out.
